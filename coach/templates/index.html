LET'S GET TO KNOW YOU

First, the basics.

A few details help make your dashboard yours. You can update these anytime.

Enter height in cm or feet and inches. Feet take precedence over cm; inches are optional and must be less than 12. Height is saved in cm, rounded to two decimal places (50–250 cm).

Enter weight in kg or lb. If both are provided, lb takes precedence; it is converted to kg and rounded to two decimal places. Accepted weights are 20–350 kg after conversion.

FIND YOUR RHYTHM

Let's talk power.

Your threshold helps translate effort into personal training zones.

Enter FTP or a typical zone 2 wattage. If both are provided, FTP takes precedence. Zone 2 is assumed to be the midpoint (65.5%) of FTP, so the resulting FTP is only an estimate.
